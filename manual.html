Getting Started
  1. 1
    Unbox your device. Carefully open the box and remove the SmogSpy Air Purifier. Inside you will find the main unit, a power adapter, a filter cartridge, and a quick start guide.
  2. 2
    Remove packaging materials. Peel off all protective plastic film from the unit's surface and remove the foam inserts from the air inlet and outlet vents before operating.
  3. 3
    Place the unit on a flat surface. Ensure at least 20cm of clearance on all sides for optimal airflow.
  4. 4
    Connect to power. Plug the DC barrel jack into the power input port and connect to a 6V–9V DC adapter.
  5. 5
    Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds until the indicator light turns blue.

Important: Do not operate near water or in areas of high humidity. Keep out of reach of children under 3 years of age.

How to Use
  1. 1
    Auto Mode: In Auto Mode, the built-in CO₂ and humidity sensors continuously monitor your room's air quality and automatically adjust the fan speed to maintain a healthy environment.
  2. 2
    Manual Speed Control: Press the fan speed button on the top panel to cycle through Low, Medium, and High settings. The current speed level is shown by the number of lit indicators on the front display.
  3. 3
    Sleep / Night Mode: Press and hold the fan button for 2 seconds to activate Sleep Mode. The unit will run at its lowest speed, dim all indicator lights, and operate near-silently for undisturbed rest.
  4. 4
    Timer Settings: Press the Timer button to set an automatic shutoff in 2, 4, or 8 hour intervals. The timer indicator on the front panel will blink once to confirm your selected duration.
  5. 5
    WiFi & Blynk App Control: Download the Blynk IoT app on Android or iOS and follow the in-app pairing guide to connect your SmogSpy device and monitor air quality remotely.
Maintenance & Filter Replacement
  1. 1
    Pre-filter cleaning (every 2 weeks): Remove the back panel by pressing the release tabs on both sides. Pull out the pre-filter mesh and gently tap it to remove dust, or rinse with water and allow it to fully dry before reinserting.
  2. 2
    HEPA filter replacement (every 6–12 months): With the back panel removed, slide out the HEPA filter cartridge by pulling the tab upward. Insert the new cartridge with the airflow arrow pointing inward, then reattach the back panel until it clicks.
  3. 3
    Carbon filter replacement (every 3 months): The carbon filter sits beneath the HEPA cartridge. Lift it out using the pull tab and replace with a new one. A noticeable reduction in odor absorption is a sign the carbon filter needs replacing sooner.
  4. 4
    Reset filter indicator: After replacing any filter, hold the Filter button for 5 seconds to reset the indicator light.

Note: Only use genuine SmogSpy replacement filters. Third-party filters may void your warranty and reduce performance.
